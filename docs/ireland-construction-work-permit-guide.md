## 1. Executive summary

Ireland is a strong option for an experienced construction professional because its 2026 employment permit updates opened more construction roles to non-EEA workers.

The biggest opportunity from the 2026 update:

- **Construction Planner / Construction Scheduler** added to the Critical Skills list.
- **Geospatial Surveyor / Land Surveyor / Geomatics Surveyor** added to the Critical Skills list.
- **Steel Fixers**, **Fencing Operators/Erectors**, **Curtain Wallers**, **Plastic Lining Technicians**, and **Concrete Pump Operators** removed from the Ineligible Occupations List and now eligible for General Employment Permits.

For someone with 10+ years of construction experience, Ireland strategy depends on role:

- If the person has planning, scheduling, surveying, project controls, BIM, or project-management experience, target Critical Skills roles.
- If the person is a skilled trade worker such as steel fixer, concrete pump operator, curtain wall installer, or related construction specialist, target General Employment Permit roles.
- If the person is a construction manager, site manager, project manager, quantity surveyor, BIM manager, or engineer, check whether the role maps to the Critical Skills list or another permit-eligible role.

## 2. Ireland permit route ranking

### Route 1: Critical Skills Employment Permit

This is the best route where eligible.

Why:

- No Labour Market Needs Test.
- Better long-term residence pathway.
- Can lead to Stamp 4 permission after the required period.
- More attractive to skilled candidates and families.

Relevant construction roles include:

- Construction Planner;
- Construction Scheduler;
- Geospatial Surveyor;
- Land Surveyor;
- Geomatics Surveyor;
- Construction Project Manager;
- Quantity Surveyor;
- BIM Manager;
- Engineering roles such as civil, structural, site, mechanical, electrical, project, process, planning or quality engineering where applicable.

2026 salary note:

- Standard Critical Skills threshold is EUR 40,904 for listed occupations with relevant degree/qualification.
- Higher-remuneration route may apply for some eligible roles above EUR 68,911.
- Always verify current thresholds before application.

Best candidate fit:

- planner/scheduler with Primavera P6/MS Project/project controls experience;
- surveyor with geospatial/land surveying experience;
- construction manager with major project history;
- quantity surveyor/commercial manager;
- BIM/project controls/site engineering profile.

### Route 2: General Employment Permit

This is the main route for many construction trades and roles not on the Critical Skills list.

Why:

- Covers occupations that are not on the Ineligible Occupations List.
- The 2026 changes opened more construction trade roles.
- Can support long-term residence after years of lawful work.

Key 2026 construction roles now accessible:

- Steel Fixer;
- Fencing Operator / Erector;
- Curtain Waller / Curtain Wall Installer;
- Concrete Pump Operator;
- Plastic Lining Technician.

General Employment Permit requirements usually include:

- job offer from a bona fide Irish employer;
- role not ineligible;
- minimum salary threshold, generally EUR 36,605 from 1 March 2026 for most roles;
- Labour Market Needs Test by employer, unless exempt;
- 50/50 workforce rule for employer;
- relevant experience/qualification;
- employment permit application through Employment Permits Online.

### Route 3: Work first, long-term residence later

For General Employment Permit workers:

- permit may be granted initially and renewed;
- after 5 years of legal residence/work, the worker may be able to apply for longer-term immigration permission, subject to Irish rules.

This is slower than Critical Skills but still realistic.

## 4. Step-by-step Ireland plan

### Step 1: Choose correct role category

Decide whether the candidate is:

- planner/scheduler/project controls;
- construction manager/site manager;
- surveyor;
- skilled construction trade;
- construction engineer;
- general labour/supervisor.

This determines CSEP vs GEP.

### Step 2: Prepare Ireland-ready CV

CV should show:

- 10+ years construction experience;
- project types;
- role title;
- tools/equipment/software;
- safety record;
- drawings/specifications experience;
- team size;
- project value;
- international standards;
- trade certificates;
- references.

### Step 3: Apply to employers

Target employers that understand permits and international hiring.

### Step 4: Confirm permit fit before accepting

Ask employer:

- Will this role qualify for Critical Skills or General Employment Permit?
- Will the company support the employment permit application?
- Has Labour Market Needs Test been completed, if GEP?
- Does the salary meet the threshold?
- Is the company compliant with the 50/50 rule?
- Is the contract duration enough?

### Step 5: Employment permit application

Either employer or employee can apply, but employer-supported applications are often smoother.

### Step 6: Entry visa from Nigeria

After employment permit approval, Nigerian citizens generally need the correct Irish entry visa before travel.

### Step 7: Arrival and registration

After arrival, complete Irish immigration registration as required.

## 6. Ireland CV strategy

Use a 2-page CV for trade roles and up to 3 pages for senior project/construction manager roles.

Include:

- target job title;
- years of experience;
- trade/management specialty;
- project list;
- tools/software;
- certifications;
- safety training;
- work permit note.

Work permit note:

> Nigerian citizen. Requires Irish employment permit and entry visa. Open to roles eligible under Critical Skills Employment Permit or General Employment Permit, depending on job title, salary, and employer eligibility.

### CV bullet examples

- Managed construction programme for [project type], coordinating [number] workers/subcontractors.
- Planned weekly/monthly site schedule using [tool], reducing delays by [result].
- Installed/fixed reinforcement steel according to drawings and structural specifications.
- Operated concrete pump safely across [project type], meeting pour schedule and safety requirements.
- Installed curtain wall systems in accordance with facade drawings and quality standards.
- Coordinated site safety, toolbox talks, materials, labour and inspections.

## 9. Best Ireland strategy for this candidate

If the candidate has planning/scheduling experience:

1. Target Construction Planner/Scheduler under Critical Skills.
2. Highlight Primavera P6/MS Project/project controls.
3. Apply to large contractors and engineering consultancies.

If the candidate is a skilled trade worker:

1. Target GEP-eligible construction roles such as steel fixer, curtain waller, concrete pump operator.
2. Apply to JobsIreland and specialist construction recruiters.
3. Ensure salary meets GEP threshold.

If the candidate is a site manager/project manager:

1. Target companies with major housing, infrastructure, data centre, pharma or energy projects.
2. Confirm role can fit Critical Skills or GEP.
3. Use recruiter support to find employers familiar with permits.
