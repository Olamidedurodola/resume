## 2. Age impact for Ireland

Ireland is much less age-sensitive than Canada or Australia.

Key point:

- At age 40-42, he is still viable for Ireland if he secures a qualifying job offer.
- Ireland does not use an Express Entry-style age points system for work permits.
- The main issues are job title, salary, employer eligibility, contract, permit route and qualifications/experience.

This makes Ireland a strong practical target for him, especially if Australia becomes risky due to age or VETASSESS qualification matching.

## 3. 2026 Ireland construction permit opportunity

The supplied Nairametrics article reports 32 employment-permit changes in Ireland, including construction-sector openings.

Important construction changes:

### Added to Critical Skills Employment Permit list

- Construction Planner;
- Construction Scheduler;
- Geospatial Surveyor / Land Surveyor / Geomatics Surveyor.

### Removed from Ineligible Occupations List and now eligible under General Employment Permit

- Steel Fixer;
- Fencing Operator / Erector;
- Curtain Waller / Curtain Wall Installer;
- Concrete Pump Operator;
- Plastic Lining Technician.

What this means:

- If he can credibly present as a construction planner, scheduler, site manager, project controls person, construction manager or supervisor, he should target higher-skilled permit roles first.
- If his hands-on experience is stronger in trade/site execution, he should target GEP construction roles now newly accessible.

## 4. Best Ireland route ranking for him

### Route 1: Critical Skills Employment Permit - best if role fits

Best target roles:

- Construction Planner;
- Construction Scheduler;
- Project Planner;
- Project Controls Planner;
- Primavera P6 Planner;
- Construction Manager;
- Site Manager;
- Construction Project Manager;
- Setting Out Engineer, if qualified;
- Quantity Surveyor, if qualified;
- BIM Coordinator/Manager, if qualified.

Why it is best:

- no Labour Market Needs Test;
- stronger long-term pathway;
- Stamp 4 route after the required period;
- better family pathway.

Risk:

- He must show the role genuinely requires his level of construction planning/project/site management expertise.
- If he does not have planning/scheduling/project controls evidence, CSEP may be difficult.

### Route 2: General Employment Permit - strong practical route

Best target roles:

- Steel Fixer;
- Concrete Pump Operator;
- Curtain Wall Installer;
- Fencing Erector;
- Mechanical Site Fitter;
- Welder-Fabricator;
- Formwork Carpenter;
- Groundworker;
- Plant Operator;
- Construction Supervisor where not ineligible.

Why it works:

- 2026 changes make more construction roles permit-eligible.
- His 10+ years experience is valuable.
- GEP is broad as long as the role is not ineligible.

Key requirements:

- minimum salary generally EUR 36,605 from 1 March 2026 for most GEP roles;
- Labour Market Needs Test by employer;
- employer must satisfy 50/50 workforce rule;
- job offer from genuine Irish employer;
- application through Employment Permits Online.

### Route 3: Skilled job first, long-term residence later

If he enters under GEP, long-term residence is slower than CSEP but still possible after sustained lawful residence/work, subject to Irish immigration rules.

## 7. Step-by-step Ireland plan

### Step 1: Choose target track

Track A - CSEP:

- Construction Planner/Scheduler;
- Construction Manager/Site Manager;
- Project Controls.

Track B - GEP:

- Steel Fixer;
- Curtain Wall Installer;
- Concrete Pump Operator;
- Site Fitter;
- Welder-Fabricator;
- hands-on construction roles.

### Step 2: Build Ireland CV

Use a construction CV, not a general CV.

Include:

- target title;
- 10+ years experience;
- UK construction experience;
- NVQ Site Supervisor;
- project list;
- safety record;
- tools/software;
- visa note.

Visa note:

> Nigerian citizen. Requires Irish employment permit and entry visa. Open to roles eligible under Critical Skills Employment Permit or General Employment Permit, depending on job title, salary and employer eligibility.

### Step 3: Apply to employers and recruiters

Target large contractors first because they understand permits better.

### Step 4: Ask permit questions early

Ask:

- Is this role eligible for CSEP or GEP?
- Can the company support an employment permit?
- Does the salary meet the current threshold?
- Has Labour Market Needs Test been completed if GEP?
- Is the company compliant with the 50/50 rule?

### Step 5: Secure offer and permit

After job offer:

- employer or candidate applies for employment permit;
- wait for approval;
- apply for Irish visa from Nigeria if required;
- travel;
- register immigration permission.
