# Kuwait Direct Jobs Guide for Nigerians Without Paying Agents

Prepared for: Nigerians seeking Kuwait jobs directly
Target country: Kuwait
Primary pathway: Direct employer contact, employer-sponsored Kuwait work visa, Wafid medical, police report, embassy stamping, and post-arrival residence processing
Research date: 31 May 2026

> This guide is practical job-search and relocation preparation material, not legal advice. Kuwait work visa rules, embassy procedures, Wafid medical requirements, costs, and processing times can change. Always verify details with the Kuwait employer, Wafid, the Kuwait Embassy in Abuja, and official government channels before paying for any service or travelling.

## 1. Executive summary

Many Nigerians pay agents very large amounts, sometimes reported around NGN 3 million to NGN 5 million, for Kuwait jobs. The direct route is to find real Kuwait job postings yourself, contact the employer or recruiter directly, interview, secure a real offer, and let the Kuwait employer sponsor the work visa process.

The direct process is:

1. Search Kuwait job sites and official employer career pages.
2. Shortlist jobs with clear contact details and realistic salaries.
3. Message the recruiter or HR contact by WhatsApp or email.
4. Send a clean PDF CV/resume and passport-ready details only when the contact is verified.
5. Interview by WhatsApp call, video call, Zoom, Google Meet, or phone.
6. Receive a written offer and confirm employer legitimacy.
7. Employer processes Kuwait work permit/entry visa.
8. Employer sends a **PDF copy** of the Kuwait e-visa/work entry visa, not just a screenshot or photo.
9. Book Wafid medical appointment and complete medical at the assigned centre.
10. Get police character certificate/report.
11. Complete required document stamping/attestation and embassy visa stamping in Abuja.
12. Travel to Kuwait.
13. Complete another medical examination, biometrics, residence permit/Civil ID processing, and final onboarding in Kuwait.

Practical cost note:

- The user-supplied field estimate as at 31 May 2026 is around **NGN 500,000** for the legitimate process and bureaucracy after getting a real job offer. This is not an official fee schedule. It can change based on medical fees, police report, transport, embassy handling, document authentication, exchange rate, passport status, and whether an employer covers some costs.

Practical timeline note:

- Nigeria-side processing may take around one month after the employer sends the visa paperwork, if documents are complete and medical/embassy appointments move smoothly.
- After arrival in Kuwait, the worker may undergo another medical and residence process. Some workers may wait weeks or up to about three months before fully starting work, depending on employer onboarding, medical results, Civil ID/residence processing, and company deployment schedules.

## 2. Important warning: direct does not mean careless

Direct application is good because it avoids exploitative agents. But Kuwait job classifieds can still contain fake recruiters, middlemen, duplicate posts, and scammers.

Never pay:

- "job slot" fee;
- "guaranteed visa" fee;
- "interview pass" fee;
- "sponsorship reservation" fee;
- large agent service charge;
- money to a personal bank account for visa processing;
- money before seeing a verifiable offer and employer details.

Be careful even when a recruiter uses WhatsApp. WhatsApp is common in the Gulf, but a real job process should still include:

- real company name;
- real Kuwait phone number;
- proper job title;
- written salary and benefits;
- interview or voice/video conversation;
- official company email or document;
- PDF visa/work entry document later in the process;
- employer that can be independently searched online.

## 6. Kuwait phone and WhatsApp method

Kuwait country code is:

`+965`

If the job post shows a local Kuwait number without country code, add `+965` before the number.

Examples:

- Posted number: `55512345`
- WhatsApp format: `+96555512345`

If the number already starts with `+965`, do not add it again.

## 13. After offer: Kuwait work visa sequence

The employer normally handles Kuwait-side sponsorship.

Typical sequence:

1. Employer collects passport copy, CV, photo, and possibly educational/experience documents.
2. Employer applies for work permit/approval in Kuwait through relevant authorities.
3. Employer receives approval/entry visa/e-visa/work visa document.
4. Employer sends the worker the **PDF copy** of the visa document.
5. Worker in Nigeria uses the PDF for medical/embassy processing.

Important:

- Do not accept only a photo or screenshot if the embassy/process requires PDF.
- Save the PDF in email, phone, cloud storage, and print copies.
- Check name, passport number, employer name, and visa details.

## 15. Wafid medical appointment

Official Wafid links:

- Book appointment: https://wafid.com/en/book-appointment/
- Search/check slip: https://wafid.com/en/search-slip/

Steps:

1. Go to Wafid.
2. Choose country as Nigeria.
3. Choose destination as Kuwait.
4. Choose visa type as Work Visa.
5. Enter passport and personal details exactly as on passport.
6. Pay and generate appointment slip.
7. Print the Wafid slip.
8. Attend the assigned medical centre.
9. Complete tests.
10. Check result online.

Documents usually needed:

- original passport;
- passport photocopy;
- Wafid appointment slip;
- passport photos;
- Kuwait visa copy/PDF if required;
- national ID if requested;
- payment/receipt;
- any extra documents shown on the Wafid slip or medical centre instruction.

Important:

- Use the official Wafid site, not random WhatsApp agents.
- The system may assign the medical centre.
- Medical report validity is limited; many public guides cite around 60 days for Kuwait work visa medicals, but always follow the validity shown on your official report.
- If medical expires before stamping/travel, you may need to repeat it.

## 16. Police report / police character certificate

You may need a police character certificate/report.

Official Nigeria Police POSSAP portal:

- https://www.possap.gov.ng

Practical steps:

1. Apply for Police Character Certificate/Police Clearance through POSSAP.
2. Complete the online application and payment.
3. Select the physical police branch/location for biometric capture.
4. Wait for online verification/processing instructions.
5. Go to the physical branch you selected for capturing.
6. Complete biometric capture and any required in-person verification.
7. Collect or receive the police character report/certificate as instructed.
8. Authenticate or stamp it where required, such as Ministry of Foreign Affairs or other required authority, depending on current Kuwait Embassy instruction.
9. Keep original and copies.

Do this early because police reports, capture appointments, and authentication can delay the process.

## 17. Embassy stamping in Abuja

The Kuwait Embassy in Nigeria is commonly referenced as being in Abuja. Public sources list:

- Address: 3501 Ganges Street, off Alvan Ikoku Way, Maitama District, Abuja.

Before going:

- call/confirm current address and opening hours;
- confirm whether appointment is required;
- confirm accepted documents;
- confirm fees;
- confirm whether medical report needs stamping;
- confirm police report authentication requirements;
- confirm whether they accept the e-visa PDF printout;
- confirm passport collection process.

Likely documents may include:

- passport;
- Kuwait e-visa/work entry visa PDF;
- visa application form if required;
- passport photos;
- Wafid medical report;
- police report;
- employment contract/offer;
- educational/experience certificates if required;
- payment receipts;
- copies of all documents.

The embassy or its process may call/contact the applicant when the passport is ready.

## 19. After arrival in Kuwait

After reaching Kuwait:

1. Employer receives worker.
2. Worker may stay in company accommodation.
3. Employer arranges in-country medical.
4. Worker completes biometrics/fingerprint.
5. Residence permit/Iqama/Civil ID process begins.
6. Worker waits for final clearance/deployment.
7. Worker starts work after company confirms legal clearance and site readiness.

Important:

- Do not disappear from employer accommodation.
- Keep copies of passport/visa.
- Know your company contact and accommodation address.
- Ask for written salary start date.
- Ask whether salary starts from arrival, training, or work deployment.
- Keep records of all signed documents.
