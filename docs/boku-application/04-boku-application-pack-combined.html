Admissions Committee
Master in Water Management and Environmental Engineering (UH 066 447)

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

I am writing to apply for the Master programme in Water Management and Environmental Engineering at BOKU University. I hold a B.Sc. in Agricultural and Environmental Engineering from the University of Ibadan, Nigeria (graduated March 2024, CGPA 3.18/4.00), and I am motivated to deepen my engineering training in water resources, environmental processes, and sustainable infrastructure — skills that Nigeria and many developing regions urgently need.

Why Water Management and Environmental Engineering

Growing up and studying in Nigeria, I have seen how closely water availability, agricultural productivity, and environmental degradation are linked. In many rural communities, irrigation systems are under-designed, drainage is inadequate, floods destroy farmland, and post-harvest losses remain high because water and environmental infrastructure are not managed sustainably. These challenges shaped my decision to study agricultural and environmental engineering.

During my undergraduate studies, I built a foundation in hydrology, hydraulics, irrigation engineering, drainage engineering, soil and water conservation, soil mechanics, and structural design. Courses such as Hydrology for Agricultural and Environmental Engineers, Principles of Hydrology, Irrigation and Drainage, Irrigation Engineering, and Drainage Engineering introduced me to catchment processes, watershed management, open-channel flow, and field drainage — competencies that align directly with the WMEE curriculum at BOKU.

My final-year thesis applied image processing and machine learning to agricultural quality assessment. I now want to direct those quantitative skills toward water and environmental systems, where modelling and monitoring can support better planning and more resilient infrastructure.

Why BOKU

BOKU's programme combines rigorous engineering training with a clear environmental and social perspective. I am drawn to the integration of hydrology, hydraulic engineering, water management planning, aquatic ecology, and waste management; BOKU's emphasis on sustainable resource use and developing-country perspectives; and Vienna's position as a hub for environmental research and international organisations. I am especially interested in the Water Management and Engineering specialization.

Relevant Experience

  • Engineering internships involving design, fabrication, and field implementation of agricultural systems (Christomat, Allamit).
  • Research Assistant experience in the Department of Agricultural and Environmental Engineering, University of Ibadan.
  • Completed National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).
  • Programming and data analysis skills (Python, SQL, computer modelling) applicable to hydrological data analysis and environmental decision support.

Career Goals

After completing the programme, I intend to work as a water/environmental engineer in irrigation and drainage design, rural water management, water-related risk assessment, and sustainable agricultural water systems in Nigeria and across Africa.

Financial Plan

I am prepared to finance my studies through personal savings and family support, and I am actively seeking research and assistantship opportunities at BOKU after enrollment.

Thank you for considering my application. I have enclosed my CV, ECTS mapping document, and supporting materials.
