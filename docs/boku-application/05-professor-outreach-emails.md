## Tips for sending

1. **Send Email 1 this week** — Tuesday to Thursday morning (European time) is best.
2. **Attach only a 1-page CV PDF** — not the full application pack.
3. **Keep the email under 250 words** — professors receive many emails.
4. **Do not ask for a scholarship in the first email** — ask about supervision and RA possibilities instead.
5. **Wait 10–14 days**, then send the follow-up once.
6. **Personalize if possible** — mention one specific research area of the professor (see below).
