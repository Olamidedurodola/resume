Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

I am writing to apply for the Master programme in **Water Management and Environmental Engineering** at BOKU University. I hold a **B.Sc. in Agricultural and Environmental Engineering** from the **University of Ibadan**, Nigeria (graduated March 2024, CGPA 3.18/4.00), and I am motivated to deepen my engineering training in water resources, environmental processes, and sustainable infrastructure — skills that Nigeria and many developing regions urgently need.

## Why Water Management and Environmental Engineering

Growing up and studying in Nigeria, I have seen how closely water availability, agricultural productivity, and environmental degradation are linked. In many rural communities, irrigation systems are under-designed, drainage is inadequate, floods destroy farmland, and post-harvest losses remain high because water and environmental infrastructure are not managed sustainably. These are not abstract problems for me. They are part of the daily reality that shaped my decision to study agricultural and environmental engineering.

During my undergraduate studies at the University of Ibadan, I built a strong foundation in the subjects central to your programme: **hydrology, hydraulics, irrigation engineering, drainage engineering, soil and water conservation, soil mechanics, and structural design**. Courses such as *Hydrology for Agricultural and Environmental Engineers*, *Principles of Hydrology, Irrigation and Drainage*, *Irrigation Engineering*, and *Drainage Engineering* introduced me to catchment processes, watershed management, open-channel flow, irrigation system design, and field drainage — competencies that align directly with the WMEE curriculum at BOKU.

My final-year thesis applied image processing and machine learning to agricultural quality assessment. While this project was focused on post-harvest technology, it strengthened my ability to work with data, build models, and translate field observations into analytical frameworks. I now want to direct those quantitative skills toward **water and environmental systems**, where modelling and monitoring can support better planning and more resilient infrastructure.

## Why BOKU

BOKU's Master programme in Water Management and Environmental Engineering stands out because it combines rigorous engineering training with a clear environmental and social perspective. I am particularly drawn to three aspects of the programme:

**First**, the curriculum integrates hydrology, hydraulic engineering, water management planning, aquatic ecology, and waste management within a coherent engineering framework. This matches my background and the direction I want to take professionally.

**Second**, BOKU's emphasis on sustainable resource use and developing-country perspectives resonates with my experience in Nigeria. Modules addressing water-related risks — including floods, droughts, and rural water management — reflect challenges I have observed in agricultural landscapes and smallholder farming systems.

**Third**, BOKU's international reputation in natural resources and life sciences, combined with Vienna's position as a hub for environmental research and international organisations, offers an environment where I can grow into a practitioner capable of working across Nigeria, Europe, and global development contexts.

I am especially interested in the **Water Management and Engineering** specialization, where I hope to further develop skills in hydrology, hydraulic structures, rural water systems, and sustainable water infrastructure.

## Relevant Experience

Beyond coursework, I have practical experience that supports my application:

- As an **engineering intern** at Christomat Construction Works and Services Limited, I contributed to the design and fabrication of agricultural equipment and a multi-grain drying system, gaining hands-on experience in engineering design, fabrication, and field implementation.

- At **Allamit Nigeria Limited**, I worked on the design and fabrication of agricultural processing machinery, which reinforced my understanding of how engineering solutions must function under real operational constraints.

- As a **Research Assistant** in the Department of Agricultural and Environmental Engineering at the University of Ibadan, I completed structured research involving data analysis and technical reporting.

- Through the **National Youth Service Corps (NYSC)**, I completed national service as a mathematics tutor, which strengthened my communication skills and sense of responsibility to contribute to society.

In addition, I have developed strong programming and data analysis skills in Python, SQL, and computer modelling. I see these as complementary tools for modern water and environmental engineering — for example, in hydrological data analysis, system simulation, and decision support — and I hope to apply them during my master's thesis at BOKU.

## Career Goals

After completing the programme, I intend to work as an environmental/water resources engineer in roles related to **irrigation and drainage design, rural water management, water-related risk assessment, and sustainable agricultural water systems**. In the long term, I hope to contribute to engineering consultancy, research, or development projects in Nigeria and across Africa, and to support institutions working on food security, land and water conservation, and climate-resilient infrastructure.

I also aim to maintain strong links between applied engineering practice and data-driven analysis, so that solutions are not only technically sound but also practical for the communities they serve.
