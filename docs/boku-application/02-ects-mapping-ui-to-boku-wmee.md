## 1. Purpose

This document maps courses completed during the B.Sc. Agricultural and Environmental Engineering programme at the University of Ibadan to the three knowledge areas required for admission to BOKU's Master programme in Water Management and Environmental Engineering (WMEE), as defined in the programme curriculum (§2 Admission Requirements).

**BOKU requirement:** Proof of **20 ECTS in each** of the following areas:

1. **Natural sciences** — mathematics, statistics, physics, chemistry, geology, soil science, botany, hydrobiology  
2. **Technical sciences** — geometry, mechanics, statics and strength of materials, surveying, structural design, geotechnics, geoinformation  
3. **Environmental / water engineering** — hydraulics, hydrology, river basin management, hydraulic engineering, settlement water management, waste management, transport, rural water management, spatial planning, construction economics

---

## 2. ECTS Conversion Methodology

The University of Ibadan uses **credit units (U)** rather than ECTS. Course syllabi list **lecture hours (HL)**, **practical hours (HP)**, and credit units.

**Conversion used in this document:**

> **Estimated ECTS = UI Credit Units × 1.5**

This reflects the workload of UI Faculty of Technology engineering courses, which typically include substantial laboratory/practical components (HP). Where syllabus contact hours are listed, the estimate is cross-checked against total workload (contact hours + independent study) using the European standard of **25–30 hours per ECTS**.

*Supporting documents attached with the application: official transcript, UI curriculum/module handbook, and course outlines with HL/HP values.*
